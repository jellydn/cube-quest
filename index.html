🔤 Move Guide

Each letter tells you which face to turn. Let's learn them!

R
Right

Turn the right face clockwise

L
Left

Turn the left face clockwise

U
Up

Turn the top face clockwise

D
Down

Turn the bottom face clockwise

F
Front

Turn the front face clockwise

B
Back

Turn the back face clockwise

⟲
The apostrophe (')

Means turn counter-clockwise instead of clockwise.

R' = Right face counter-clockwise

②
The number 2

Means turn the face twice (180 degrees).

R2 = Right face turned twice

💡 Quick tip: Say the moves out loud as you do them! "Right, Up, Right-prime, Up-prime"

💡 Tips for Kids

Follow these tips and you'll be a cube master in no time!

📅

One step a day

Learn one step each day instead of trying everything at once. Your brain needs time to remember!

⏱️

Practice 10–15 minutes daily

Short practice every day is better than a long session once a week. 10 to 15 minutes is perfect!

🗣️

Say the moves aloud

Saying the moves helps you remember them. Try it!

  • R = Right
  • L = Left
  • U = Up
  • F = Front
  • ' = Counter-clockwise
  • 2 = Turn twice
🧘

Don't rush

Going slow is okay! Focus on getting it right, not getting it fast. Speed comes later!

🎉

Celebrate small wins

Finished the white cross? Awesome! Be proud of each step. Every step gets you closer to solving the whole cube!

🏆

You can do it!

Most kids can solve the cube by themselves after 3–7 days of short daily practice. Stick with it and you'll get there!
